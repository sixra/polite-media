When the poster is not frame 0

Both boxes hold the same video and the same poster, and that poster is deliberately a different picture from the video's first frame: colour bars against the clip's own pattern. The left one cuts, which is the shipped default. The right one crossfades over 600ms, because its container sets --polite-fade. Watch the swap and decide which you want.

The measured case against a frame-0 poster is different and is why the default is a cut: there, a fade blends a still against a frame the video has already moved past, so you get a double exposure of one scene. Between two genuinely different pictures it is an ordinary dissolve.
