Waiting until it can play through

requireBuffered holds playback until the browser reports it can play the video to the end without stalling. It is for thin connections, where the alternative is a video that starts and then hitches its way through the first few seconds.

It has to raise preload from none to auto to do that. Under preload="none" the browser buffers nothing until playback is requested, so waiting for canplaythrough without the promotion would wait forever. The markup below still ships preload="none"; the value on the live element is the library's doing.

On a fast local server this resolves almost at once. Throttle the network in devtools to watch the poster hold. If the buffer never fills, the poster simply stays, which is the same outcome as reduced motion or Save-Data.
