A poster that carries meaning

Once a video reveals, its poster is hidden with opacity rather than visibility, so the poster's alt text stays in the accessibility tree. The right poster is a lazy image that revealImages() fades in, with image.css loaded after video.css and the video stacked beneath it: the arrangement where a poster left opaque would hide the video.

Two more posters stay hidden under that arrangement: one inside a <picture> with display: contents, and one marked data-polite-reveal that no revealImages() call manages, the mistake the console names, so image.css's failsafe has already shown it.
