Warming what the next page will show

Hover or focus a card and watch the network panel: one request goes out, for the one variant this viewport and this browser would have chosen anyway. Nothing here parses sizes or compares a w descriptor. The candidates are handed to a detached <picture> and the browser picks, which is the same algorithm the destination runs.

Narrow the window below 50rem and reload: the warmed URL changes with it, because sizes is passed through untouched rather than reimplemented.
