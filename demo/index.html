polite-media demos

Run pnpm build and pnpm fixtures first, then serve the repo root. Media here is synthetic, generated by scripts/make-fixtures.sh.

  • hero: reveal on a genuinely presented frame
  • interaction: opting out of the default, to protect LCP
  • until: hold a hero behind a splash screen or a dialog
  • buffered: wait for the whole clip, not just the start
  • grid: arbitration and hysteresis, the hard case
  • feed: one video at a time everywhere, buffered ahead
  • art-directed poster: cut versus crossfade, side by side
  • layering: the optional stacking stylesheet
  • sizes: one video, four boxes, no imposed geometry
  • fallback: a broken source falling through
  • images: reveal on decode, and the eager guard
  • warming: fetch the next page's hero before the click
  • reveal failsafe: a marked image nothing manages
  • without scripting: marked images stay visible with JS off
  • rVFC spike: playing against the first presented frame
