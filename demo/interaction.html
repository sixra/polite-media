Waiting for the visitor

This page opts into startWhen: 'interaction'. The poster sits there until you scroll, tap or press a key, and only then does the video begin. Reach for it when Largest Contentful Paint matters: the browser stops measuring LCP on exactly those signals, so a video revealed afterwards can never become the LCP element, and an audit that never interacts never starts it at all.

The shipped default is 'page-loaded', which plays without being asked. The trade is that a visitor who lands and never moves sees a still, which on a desktop can last as long as they read.
