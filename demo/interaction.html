The default: wait for the visitor

Nothing is configured on this page, so it shows what a consumer gets out of the box. The poster sits there until you scroll, tap or press a key, and only then does the video begin. That is deliberate: the browser stops measuring Largest Contentful Paint on exactly those signals, so a video revealed afterwards can never become the LCP element, and an audit that never interacts never starts it at all. Set startWhen: 'page-loaded' if you would rather it came alive on its own.
