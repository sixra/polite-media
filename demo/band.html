A band, not a line

playAbove: 0.6 with pauseBelow: 0.3. A stopped video has to clear 60% visible to start; once running it keeps going until it drops below 30%. Because the two crossings are in different places, a video parked near the boundary cannot flicker between states.

Scroll slowly and watch the readout. The interesting range is between the two thresholds: at around half visible the video does whatever it was already doing, which is the entire point. Coming up from below it is still stopped; coming back down from fully visible it is still playing.

The readout can lag the true figure slightly. An IntersectionObserver reports only when a threshold is crossed, not continuously, so the library acts on the last crossing rather than on the pixel-accurate number here.
